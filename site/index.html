Climate risk register and prioritization matrix

A screening ranking of exposure, not a consequence assessment. Each row is one hazard meeting one value area. Exposure is measured; the exposure class comes from stated bins; the likelihoods are defaults that an assessment sets with the City. Change them below and the ranking recomputes.

Rows by likelihood and exposure class

Screening score = default likelihood × exposure class. Low 1 to 4, Medium 5 to 9, High 10 to 16, Very high 17 to 25. Rows with no exposure are not plotted.
